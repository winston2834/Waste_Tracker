**1. What is LeftoverLab?**


 LeftoverLab is a campus food-waste tracking platform. It measures how much food gets thrown away in the college mess (dining hall) and at every cafeteria shop, turns that into simple dashboards, and lets students anonymously rate food quality — so mess staff, shop owners, and campus admins can see where waste is happening and act on it. The platform is built around three simple ideas: • Log it — staff record how much food is wasted, item by item, after every meal or shift. • See it — dashboards turn those logs into daily totals, weekly trends, and (in the admin view) a short-term forecast. • Hear it — students rate meals anonymously, giving staff direct feedback on what's working and what isn't. 2.
